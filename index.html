SonoVizio is an early-stage research project to help people with low vision or blindness access visual information through sound. Your real experience shapes what we build next.

To achieve our mission we work in three directions:

The technical approach may evolve. The mission stays the same: information shouldn’t be locked behind eyesight.

Hear visual information

It’s about making direct conversion of visual information into sound and leaving your brain to figure out how it interprets it.
When trained, you'll be able to perceive any type of visual information - from images to videos.

Navigate space more confidently

It's about usable cues that help you keep orientation and move with more confidence — especially in unfamiliar places.
You will hear objects around you, their distance, and movement — all in real time.

Make computer interactions comfortable

We aim for convenient and fast interaction: clearer focus, fewer traps, less guessing, more control. You will hear interface elements, their state, and changes — all in real time.

If you’ve ever felt that tools were technically accessible, but painful in reality, your perspective can shape what we build next.

FAQ

If something isn’t clear, email us — we’ll improve the page.

Is this a medical device or a “vision cure”?

No. SonoVizio is an accessibility project. It does not claim to restore vision.

Do I need special hardware to join?

No. Joining starts remotely and can be done on a regular computer. Future concepts may explore wearables, but participation is always optional.

Who can participate?

People with low vision or blindness are the core audience. Observers and accessibility professionals can also join to help steer decisions.

How much time will it take?

You choose your level of involvement. We keep requests lightweight and optional, and we aim for minimal email frequency.

What does “lifetime access” mean?

If the platform is released, early participants will keep access without paying. The exact scope will be defined clearly before release.

Will the platform work with screen readers?

Sure, we aim for full accessibility. If anything is hard to use with assistive technology, please tell us — we will fix it.

Is this only for an academic paper?

No. The PhD structure adds accountability, but the project is built with the community and aims for real-world impact.

How often will you contact me?

At most twice per month, and only if you opt in. You can stop at any time.

Research and transparency

SonoVizio is developed as part of PhD-level research in sensory substitution and accessibility. That adds structure and accountability — but the direction is guided by community feedback.

We test assumptions with real users, document what works and what does not, and allow the technical approach to evolve as we learn.
